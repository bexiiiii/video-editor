## 4. Text animation (25 fps strips)

- **Word reveal (default, over head and on inserts)**: each word appears **in place** on its spoken onset (lead 0–0.08 s): opacity 0 → 1 + gaussian blur ≈ 16 → 0 px over **5–6 frames (0.20–0.24 s)**, near-linear (power1.out); no slide; a barely visible scale-down (≤ 1.05 → 1). - **Hook variant (rise)**: words fly up from ≈ +400 px below their slot with blur, reaching the slot in ≈ 0.3 s, ease-out (0.2 s: "кредитке" at y 1606 → 0.3 s 1380 → 0.4 s 1200 → settled 1101). Used only on the first stack.

- **Exit**: a stack normally dies **with the hard cut** (no exit animation). Inside one segment the stack is replaced by a **blur-out**: blur 0 → ≈ 18 px + fade over ≈ 7 frames (0.28 s, power1.in), then 0.25 s of clean footage, then the next stack (`tr_16.72.jpg`, 16.96–17.24 → "звонок жоқ" at 17.5).

## 6. Transitions and camera

- **Hard cuts only** (27). Head ↔ insert, head ↔ head (framing change), head ↔ B-roll: all hard, on word boundaries, usually right before the stressed word so the new framing lands with it.
- **Framing logic** (§1): wide ↔ tight alternation on each head cut, tight ≈ 1.4× about the face, back to wide after an insert. The tight crop is the "second camera".
- One **light-leak flash** (3 frames) at 56.2, marking the move into the closing argument.
- No push-ins, no whips, no zoom drift, no shake.

## 7. Captions and voice sync

- Two text layers, mutually exclusive in time: **small captions** (literal, 1–3 words per phrase, a new phrase every ≈ 0.5–1.0 s, **hard on / hard off**, whole phrase appears on the first word's onset — `tr_5.36.jpg`) and **kinetic stacks** (key phrases only, ≈ 25 % of the runtime over the head, 100 % of inserts/B-roll).
- Stack words are timed to their spoken onsets (машинасы @ 0.50 = ASR 0.50; "ол ито" @ 9.16 vs ASR 9.14); the reveal leads by 0–0.08 s, the hook rise leads by ≈ 0.2 s.
- Captions are literal (Russian loans kept as spoken, e.g. "досрочное погашениесі"); the highlight is a number or the verb of the point ("5 млн", "білмейді").
- Stacks rewrite the phrase into its key words (≤ 6 words) — not always literal word-for-word ("бастапқы жарна ретінде қалтасына ақша алады" is literal; "2-ақ вариант бар!" is literal; "звонок жоқ" literal).

## 8. Audio

- **Voice**: dry, close lav, dominant. Integrated **−14.0 LUFS**, LRA 2.4 LU; 40 ms RMS percentiles 5 / 50 / 95 % = −39 / −19 / −11 dBFS.
- **Music: none.** Between words the level falls to **−44…−46 dBFS** with every band < −70 dB (e.g. 1.66–1.86: 30–80 Hz −81, 640–1280 Hz −89) — silence, not a bed.
- **SFX: almost none.** No whooshes on cuts or stack reveals (HF bands at cuts and reveals track the voice only). One **sub-boom on the end card** (59.48–60.0: −22.6 dBFS, 30–80 Hz at −51 dB vs −74 under speech) with a short low-mid tail.
- Voice continues across inserts (inserts are visual only).

## 9. Secret sauce

1. **Two framings from one camera** cut every ≈ 2.2 s: the edit feels like a two-camera interview shoot although it is one locked-off shot cropped 1.0 / 1.4.
2. **Words as layout**: the key phrase becomes a poster — mixed sizes (XL/L/M/S), one accent colour, lines interlocking left/right — instead of a subtitle line.
3. **Two text layers that never overlap**: quiet small captions carry the speech; big stacks fire only on the point of each sentence.
4. **Flat colour inserts with one cut-out object** answer the question visually (2 pills = 2 options, a person with a burden = debt), held 1.2–2.2 s, then straight back to the face.
5. **Restraint in sound**: dry voice, no music, no whooshes — the cuts and type carry the rhythm.
6. Real proof (the marketplace page) tilted full-bleed with type on top.

**What a naive recreation gets wrong**
- Big captions on every phrase (kills the contrast between layers), or small captions under a stack.
- All lines the same size / centred / evenly spaced (no interlock), or more than one accent colour per stack.
- Bouncy pops, slides or typewriter on the stack words (the reference only blurs in, in place).
- A slow Ken-Burns zoom instead of hard framing cuts; or a punch that is too small (1.12 reads as a mistake, 1.35–1.5 reads as a second camera).
- Drop shadows / boxes behind text, gradient inserts, a grid background.
- Whooshes on every cut, or a music bed.
- A font without Kazakh glyphs (fallback mid-word on қ ғ ү ұ ө ә).
