## 4. Text animation

- **Word reveal:** each word enters with opacity 0→1, blur 10→0, plus a **horizontal slide of ~+40 px → 0** (a bigger slide than in the sibling). Over 4–5 frames (160–200 ms), expo.out. The visible gap between words closes as the word lands. Measured: "kontent?" lands 2.88→3.04, with the gap shrinking from ~60 px to normal word spacing. Words are timed to the spoken onset.
- **Selection highlight (the signature of this video):** it marks the key headline after it lands. Breakdown:
  - The start handle appears first: a 17 px #007AFF dot plus a 3 px caret line that rises above the cap-height.
  - The highlight **sweeps left→right in 5 frames** (3.24→3.44 s). The box is rgba(0,122,255,0.25), rendering ≈ #BCD9F7 on light. It is 77 px tall, from 6 px above the cap-height to just below the baseline, with padding ~14 px each side.
  - The end handle (caret + dot hanging below) fades in 4–8 frames later (3.56–3.64).
  - It is used on 4 headlines: "Qanday kontent?", "3 ta yo'nalish", "9 ta Reels g'oyasi", "real mijozlar". The selection then stays until the scene exits.
- **Strike-through:** a 2 px line draws left→right over ~0.25 s across "👀 ko'rishlar" and the word dims. The replacement headline then appears below with selection: correction → emphasis.
- **Counters:** the budget number rolls with **vertical motion-blur on the digits** (191 360 at 32.5 is blurred), ease-out over ~1 s, landing on a round "300 000". The video order counters tick up in integer steps across the 9 tiles at different speeds.
- **Typing-style text inside cards** (name, podium title): per-word blur-fade, synced to the VO.
- **Exits:** only with the scene transition or the glitch.

- **Scene transitions (8×):** 0.45–0.6 s (11–15 frames). Breakdown:
  - The outgoing scene **scales down to ~0.95 and drifts up slightly** (letterbox-like dark bars of #2A2A2A appear top and bottom at 16.80–16.92).
  - It fades out over a dark base while the incoming bg fades in.
  - The new step label or kicker starts ~0.15 s after the bg settles.
  - Measured light→blue 16.60–17.05 (old at 0.97 scale with dark edges by 16.84, fully blue by 16.96, "2-qadam" pill fades in 17.04–17.24). Ease power2.inOut.

- **Camera:** none in the graphics. The footage is locked-off.

## 7. Sync with voice

- Literal. Every on-screen word is the spoken word (or its key noun), revealed at its onset (±2 frames):
  - "Qanday" appears 2.6 vs VO "qandaq" 2.5.
  - "kontent?" 2.9 vs 2.9.
  - "3 ta" 10.5 vs "uch" 10.4.
  - "real mijozlar" 53.5 vs 53.5.
- List items appear on their spoken item. Abstract nouns get a component state change instead of text: "Yetarli" chip, winner tile, Saqlandi tap.
- Selection highlights land ~0.3–0.6 s after the headline completes. The highlight is the visual "stress" in place of a vocal emphasis.
- The CTA tap animates on the word "saqlab" and the follow pill on "obuna".

## 8. Audio

- VO is dry and close, about −14 dBFS RMS on speech. In the footage outro the same voice continues on the sync mic; the room tone matches, with no audible level jump.
- **Music:** the same sub-bass bed as the sibling. <120 Hz holds at ≈ −20 dBFS with long decays, there is essentially no HF content, and the tempo is ~99 BPM. The **bass drops out ~16.9–23.7 s** (the 2-qadam blue scene), exactly like the sibling at 17.0–23.7. It is the same music template and edit.
- **SFX:** none clearly detectable. Possibly a soft glitch noise at 55.7–56.0 is masked under the VO. For a recreation, a −24 dB digital glitch burst on the glitch transition and a soft tick on the "Saqlash" tap are safe additions.

## 9. Secret sauce & pitfalls

1. **The iOS text-selection highlight** (handles + blue 25% box, 5-frame L→R sweep) as the emphasis device. It is unique to this template and reads as "this is the key phrase".
2. **Skeleton-to-content morphs:** empty placeholders appear first (the "?" grid, skeleton rows), so the viewer feels the gap before it is filled.
3. **Segmented control as section navigator** inside a section: the active capsule moves 1→2→3 across three scenes and gives continuity across theme flips.
4. **Numbers as heroes:** "3 + 3", the rolling budget counter with motion blur, "9 ta". Big Bold digits in squircle tiles.
5. **Correction beat:** the strike-through of "ko'rishlar" leads into "real mijozlar", with selection. It's a micro-story in one scene.
6. **Real iOS idioms:** notification cards with "hozir", tap ripple, Saqlash → Saqlandi state, Apple system palette.
7. **Two blue takeovers** mark the "method" beats (2-qadam, the 3×3 matrix). The dark-grey studio-floor gradient keeps the dark scenes warm rather than black.
8. **The face appears only at the very end,** after the value is delivered. It is a trust stamp, and the glitch keeps the switch from feeling like a hard cut.

Pitfalls:
- A selection box that is fully opaque or #007AFF at 50%+. It must be ~25% so the black text stays crisp, with no text-colour change.
- Missing the handles.
- Words fading in place instead of sliding in from +40 px with blur.
- A pure-black dark bg; it's #1A1A1C at the top → #303033 at the bottom.
- Putting chrome or the grid over the footage.
- A crossfade into the footage instead of the glitch.
- Rounding the footage corners or adding a frame.
- Using non-Apple emoji.
