# Edit modes

| Mode | Picture | Audio source | Typical structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `faceless` | 100 % motion scenes | TTS (stock or cloned voice) or user's voice recording | hook scene → 8–14 motion scenes → CTA |
| `hook_cta` | talking head 2–7 s → motion body → talking head CTA 3–5 s | user footage voice | B: portal / glitch between head and motion; A: blur-drift |
| `head_overlay` | talking head full length, motion components on top | user footage voice | captions one line in chest band, components pop beside the speaker, jump-cuts + punch-ins, occasional full-screen motion insert (≤ 4 s) |

Footage expectations to tell the user: vertical 9:16, face at upper third, eyes ~38 % from top, clean audio (lav mic or phone close), pauses between thoughts (they get cut), say the hook first.
